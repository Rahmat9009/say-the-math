Use the reader with a keyboard

  1. Tab to Choose an image and press Enter. You can also paste an image with Control+V or Command+V.
  2. Choose Read equation. Your screen reader announces when the result is ready.
  3. Tab to Explore the equation. Use Up and Down to move between visible parts. Right expands a collapsed part, then enters it. Left collapses an expanded part, then returns to its parent. Home and End jump to the first and last visible parts.
  4. Press Enter in the explorer to announce the current part again. Native MathML is also available under Recognized equation. Your screen reader may need focus/forms mode to send arrow keys to the explorer.

Choose a PDF to try experimental discovery. Every page is listed: use the page selector and Previous/Next page. The candidate selector follows reading order; Previous/Next candidate can skip pages without candidates. Multi-line candidates are listed but not read. Automatic crops may be incomplete; missed regions cannot reliably be recovered without visual selection. Use an already-cropped printed-equation image as another input. Screen-reader compatibility still needs user testing.

Adjust NVDA speech

Say the Math uses your screen reader's current voice and speed. Choose what feels comfortable; this app does not change your speech preferences.

In NVDA, hold your NVDA key and Control, then press V to open Speech settings. Tab to Voice or Rate and use the arrow keys to adjust. Tab to OK and press Enter to apply, or press Escape to cancel. To choose another synthesizer, press NVDA+Control+S. Your NVDA key is Insert or Caps Lock, depending on your setup. Press Control on its own to stop speech.
